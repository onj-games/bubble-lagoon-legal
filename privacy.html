Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

ON.J ("Company," "we," "us") is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this Privacy Policy, collected through the mobile game "Bubble Lagoon" (the "Service"). Contact details for privacy matters are in Section 14.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data when you download, install, or play the Service. It applies regardless of the device or platform you use to access the Service, and supplements (without replacing) the privacy terms of the app store you downloaded it from.

3. Personal Data We Collect

3.1 Automatically Collected Data

When you access or play the Service, the following data is automatically collected and stored, for example in server access logs:

  1. Device identifier (advertising ID / IDFA / GAID)
  2. Operating system name and version
  3. Device type and model
  4. IP address
  5. Date and time of access
  6. App version and install source
  7. Approximate geolocation derived from IP address
  8. In-app interaction data (session length, level progress, taps/clicks on screens)

3.2 Data You Provide Directly

  • Information you submit when contacting customer support (e.g., email address, message content)
  • Purchase confirmation data returned by the app store after an in-app purchase (we do not receive or store your card details)

3.3 Data Collected via Third-Party SDKs

The Service integrates the following third-party SDKs, which may independently collect device and usage data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) - app usage and gameplay events, app-instance ID, device and OS information, approximate location derived from IP address
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) - crash reports and stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, installation UUID
  • Google Firebase Remote Config (Google LLC) - app-instance ID, used to deliver game configuration values
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google LLC) - advertising ID, IP address, device and OS information, ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completion), used to serve and measure rewarded and interstitial ads. Personalized ads are shown only where you have consented, where consent is required.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (Google LLC) - your consent choices for advertising, stored on your device
  • Unity In-App Purchasing / Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) - installation ID, device information, purchase receipts and transaction IDs, used to process and restore in-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads" and gold packs) through Google Play billing

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies (e.g., you are located in the EEA or UK), we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) - to provide the Service and process purchases you request
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) - to maintain security, prevent fraud, and improve the Service, balanced against your rights
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) - for optional advertising/analytics identifiers where consent is required, which you may withdraw at any time
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) - where we must retain or disclose data to comply with law

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining the Service
  • Processing in-app purchases and rewarded-ad grants
  • Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Analyzing usage to diagnose issues and improve game design and performance
  • Personalizing and measuring the effectiveness of advertising, where permitted
  • Responding to customer support requests
  • Complying with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with the following categories of recipients:

  • Advertising and analytics partners (see 3.3), to the extent you have not opted out where opt-out is available
  • App store platforms, to process purchases and deliver the Service to your device
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., hosting, customer support tooling), under contractual confidentiality obligations
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law or to protect our legal rights

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the Republic of Korea and the United States (where servers of Google and Unity Technologies are located), which may have different data protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms to protect data transferred internationally.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (e.g., tax, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations). Aggregated or de-identified data that can no longer be linked to you may be retained indefinitely.

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of your data, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9.2 If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (as those terms are defined under California law) - including opting out of cross-app advertising via your device's ad-tracking settings.

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 14.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at onj.gamez@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age threshold that applies under local law, e.g., 14 under Korean PIPA), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age without verified parental consent. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child in violation of this Policy, we will delete it promptly.

11. Cookies, SDKs, and Tracking Technologies

The Service uses device identifiers and comparable technologies (rather than browser cookies) to remember settings, measure usage, and support advertising, as described in Section 3.3. You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, or opt out of personalized ads, through your device's privacy settings (e.g., iOS App Tracking Transparency, Android ad settings). Where advertising consent is required (for example in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), you are asked for it when the game starts and can change your choice at any time via Settings > Privacy Choices in the game. If you purchase "Remove Ads," interstitial ads are no longer shown; rewarded ads you choose to watch remain available.
